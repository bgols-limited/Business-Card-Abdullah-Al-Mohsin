## Quick answer: which way to share

| Way to share | Works on | What the other person gets |
|---|---|---|
| **QR code** on the card | Any phone camera | The live digital card (call, WhatsApp, WeChat, save contact, directions) |
| **NFC tag** (sticker or card) | iPhone XS or newer, Android with NFC on | The live digital card |
| **NameDrop** (hold the iPhones together) | iPhone to iPhone, iOS 17.1+ | Name, chosen numbers and email, photo / Contact Poster |
| **AirDrop** the contact or the link | iPhone to iPhone or Mac | Full contact or the live card |
| **Quick Share** the link | Android to Android (Samsung and Pixel) | The live digital card |
| **Share Profile** button (WhatsApp, Telegram, email, SMS) | Any phone | The card link |

A phone cannot act as an NFC tag by itself, and a web page cannot send by NFC. So "tap my card"
means a cheap **NFC tag**, and "tap my phone" means **NameDrop** (iPhone only).

## If he uses an iPhone

### 1. Put the card on the Home Screen (1 minute)

1. Open **https://alshams8.github.io/Business-Card-Abdullah-Al-Mohsin/** in **Safari** (not Chrome).
2. Tap the **Share** button (square with an arrow), scroll down, tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. The icon "A. Al Mohsin" now opens the card like an app, also offline.

### 2. Save the contact and make it his "My Card" (once, 3 minutes)

1. In the card, tap **Save Contact**, then **Create New Contact**, then **Done**.
   The contact has his photo, both numbers, email, title, the three office addresses, website and card link.
2. Open **Settings › Apps › Contacts › My Info** (iOS 18; on iOS 17: **Settings › Contacts › My Info**)
   and choose **Abdullah Al Mohsin**.
3. Open the **Contacts** app, tap **My Card** at the top, then **Contact Photo & Poster** to set what other iPhones see.
4. Check NameDrop is on: **Settings › General › AirDrop › Bringing Devices Together**.

### 3. iPhone to iPhone: NameDrop

1. Both iPhones unlocked, on iOS 17.1 or later.
2. Hold the **top** of his iPhone a few centimetres from the top of the other iPhone.
3. When NameDrop appears, tap **Share** (swap cards) or **Receive Only**.
4. First time only: tap the arrow next to his name, choose both numbers and the email, then **Save**.

NameDrop sends only the name, chosen numbers or email, and the Contact Poster. To give the
**full** card (offices, website, card link), also use AirDrop below.

### 4. AirDrop the full contact or the link

- **Full contact:** Contacts › **My Card** › **Share Contact** › **AirDrop** › tap the other iPhone.
- **Live card link:** in the card, **Share Profile** › **Share via…** › **AirDrop**.
- The other iPhone must have AirDrop on **Contacts Only** or **Everyone for 10 Minutes**
  (Control Centre, press and hold the network box, then AirDrop).

### 5. Optional: QR in one double tap

- **Shortcuts** app › **+** › **Add Action** › **Open URLs** › enter
  **https://alshams8.github.io/Business-Card-Abdullah-Al-Mohsin/#qr** › name it "My QR".
- **Settings › Accessibility › Touch › Back Tap › Double Tap** › **My QR**.
  A double tap on the back of the iPhone now opens the card already flipped to the QR side.

## If he uses an Android phone (Samsung, Pixel, Xiaomi, and others)

### 1. Put the card on the Home screen (1 minute)

- **Chrome:** open the card address › **⋮** menu › **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**) › **Install**.
- **Samsung Internet:** open the card address › **☰** menu › **Add page to** › **Home screen**.

### 2. Save the contact (once)

1. In the card, tap **Save Contact**.
2. Open the downloaded **contact.vcf** (from the notification or the Downloads folder).
3. Choose where to save it (Google account or phone), then **Save** / **Import**.
4. Samsung only: open **Contacts**, tap **My profile** at the top and fill it the same way,
   so Samsung's own **QR code** for "My profile" can be shown too.

### 3. Show the QR code

- Open the card from the Home screen and tap the **QR** button (bottom right of the card), or **Show QR side**.
- The other person points their camera at it. Any iPhone or Android camera opens the card.
- Optional: the address with **#qr** at the end opens the QR side directly. Save **https://alshams8.github.io/Business-Card-Abdullah-Al-Mohsin/#qr** as a second Home screen shortcut called "My QR" if he likes.

### 4. NFC tag (works for iPhone and Android visitors)

1. Buy blank **NTAG215** cards or **NTAG213** stickers.
2. Easiest on Android: open **https://alshams8.github.io/Business-Card-Abdullah-Al-Mohsin/nfc-setup.html** in **Chrome**,
   tap **Write to NFC tag**, and hold the tag against the **middle of the back** of the phone.
   (Or use the free **NFC Tools** app: **Write › Add a record › URL / URI** › paste the card address › **Write**.)
3. Test: tap the tag with another phone. On Android, NFC must be on
   (**Settings › Connected devices › Connection preferences › NFC**; on Samsung **Settings › Connections › NFC and contactless payments**).
4. When it works, lock it in NFC Tools: **Other › Lock tag** (permanent).

Tip: put the sticker on the back of his phone case. Other people then tap **his case** with their phone.

### 5. Android to Android: Quick Share

1. In the card, tap **Share Profile** › **Share via…** › **Quick Share**, then tap the nearby phone.
2. To send the full contact: **Contacts** › his contact › **Share** › **Quick Share** (sends the .vcf).
3. The other phone must have Quick Share visible (pull down quick settings › **Quick Share** ›
   **Contacts only** or **Everyone for 10 minutes**).

## Troubleshooting

- **NameDrop does not appear:** both iPhones need iOS 17.1+, unlocked, and "Bringing Devices Together" on. Hold them top to top, really close.
- **Contact file does nothing on Android:** open it from **Files › Downloads** and choose **Contacts** to open it.
- **NFC tag does nothing:** the phone must be unlocked with the screen on, NFC switched on (Android). Hold for 1 to 2 seconds.
- **Card shows an old version:** close and reopen it; updates arrive on the next open.
- **WeChat:** WeChat has no public "add me" link. People search **+8801766330755** in WeChat (**+ › Add Contacts**).
  This only works if his WeChat privacy setting allows "Add me by mobile number".

Google, "Share files with Quick Share" (https://support.google.com/android/answer/9286773).
